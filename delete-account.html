Delete your SYNC account

This page explains how to delete your account in SYNC (by Meherzan Turel) and what happens to your data.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open SYNC and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile, then Settings.
  3. Under Privacy & Data, tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account and everything you created are deleted straight away, and you're signed out.

Option 2: by email (if you can't use the app)

  1. Email syncapp.noreply@gmail.com from the email address on your SYNC account, with the subject "Delete my account". If you signed in with Apple and chose "Hide my email", send it from the Apple ID email you used, and tell us that you used Sign in with Apple.
  2. To protect your account, we'll reply to confirm the request before we delete anything.
  3. We'll delete your account within 7 days of your confirmation and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your profile (name, email, photo, phone/FaceTime address, settings)Deleted immediately
Everything you created: photos, videos, voice clips, captions, moods, letters, plans, game answers, Gentle Days entries, reactionsDeleted immediately, not archived
Your devices, notification tokens, locationDeleted immediately
Your connectionEnded. Your partner is told the connection ended
Your partner's own contributions to your shared spaceArchived for your partner for 3 months, then deleted (or sooner, if they delete it)
BackupsRemoved within 7 days
Records we must keep by lawModeration records needed for an open appeal or legal claim, and any material we're legally required to preserve and report, are kept only as long as the law requires

A deleted account can't be restored. If you come back later, you'll create a new account.

Just want a break?

You don't have to delete your account to step back. You can sign out, turn off notifications, or end your connection from Settings → Disconnect partner. Your shared space is then archived for 3 months, and each of you can download what you created.
