A private space for two

Stay close,
wherever your days take you.

SYNC is a quiet, private app for couples, whether you share a city or live time zones apart. Share small moments, say "thinking of you" with a single hold, plan date nights, and play games that bring you closer.

Coming soon to the App Store.

What's inside

  • Our Day: a few photos, a voice clip or one line, shared with the one person who matters.
  • Pebbles: hold to send "thinking of you".
  • Moods & Gentle Days: let your partner know how you're doing, only when you choose.
  • Letters: write something that opens on the right day.
  • Plans: date nights, countdowns and shared dreams.
  • Play: tested games and questions for couples.

Private by design

Only you and your partner can see what you share. No ads, no trackers, no public profiles. Location sharing is off unless you turn it on, and then it's approximate.
