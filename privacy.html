Privacy Policy

SYNC is a private space for two people. This policy explains, in plain English, what we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and the choices you have.

The short version
  • What you share in SYNC is visible to you and your partner only. It isn't public, and we don't sell it or use it for advertising.
  • There are no ads, no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics in the app.
  • Location sharing is off unless you turn it on, and even then we only store an approximate (about 1 km) position.
  • Nudity and sexual content are not allowed. Uploaded photos are checked automatically on our own servers before your partner sees them. No outside moderation company sees them (see section 6).
  • You can delete your account, and what you created, from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

SYNC is provided by Meherzan Turel, Surat, Gujarat, India ("SYNC", "we", "us"). We are the controller (in India, the data fiduciary) of the personal data described here. You can reach us at syncapp.noreply@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and the name you choose. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an identifier from them and your email address. With Apple this can be a private relay address, and Apple may also share your name the first time. We never receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Profile (optional): a profile photo, a phone number and/or FaceTime address (used only to let your partner call you from the call buttons), your time zone, and preferences like your pebble colour and notification settings.
  • Pairing: invite codes, who asked to connect with whom and when, and the connection itself.
  • Things you share with your partner: photos, videos and short voice clips, captions and notes, moods, letters (including "Open when" letters and songs you attach), date-night plans and reviews, countdowns, goals, vision-board pins, playlist links, game answers and reactions, daily question answers, "Thinking of you" pebbles and other signals.
  • Gentle Days (optional): period start dates, how you're feeling on a given day, and messages for your partner (see section 4).
  • Support: whatever you send us when you contact support or appeal a decision.

Information collected automatically

  • Approximate location, only if you turn on "Share my location": your city, region, country, and a position rounded to about 1 km. Your phone works out the city itself; the precise reading never leaves your phone. We don't track location in the background.
  • Device and security data: a scrambled (hashed) identifier for each device you sign in on, its type and model name (e.g. "iPhone 15"), and when it was first and last used. We use these to warn you about sign-ins on new devices.
  • Notification data: push notification tokens for your devices, and a log of which notifications were sent (type and time only, never message content).
  • Activity status: whether you're online and when you last opened the app, shown to your partner.
  • Days you used SYNC: which days you opened the app. We only ever look at these as totals (for example, how many people used SYNC today, or how many come back after a week), never person by person, and no outside analytics company receives them.
  • Technical logs: like every online service, our hosting providers record IP addresses, timestamps and request details to run and secure the service.

What stays on your phone

When you add a date night to your calendar, SYNC writes it to your device calendar; we only store the event's ID so we can update it later. We never upload your calendar. Likewise, we only access your camera, microphone and photo library when you choose to take, record or pick something. We don't access your contacts.

We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Health information (Gentle Days)

Gentle Days lets you note period dates and how you're feeling, and choose whether your partner sees them. This is health information, which the law treats as especially sensitive. We only process it because you choose to use the feature (your explicit consent). It's private to you unless you switch on sharing in Gentle Days settings, and you can stop sharing, or delete your entries, at any time. We never use it for anything except showing it to you and, if you choose, your partner.

5. What your partner can see

SYNC is built for two. When you're connected, your partner can see:

  • your name, profile photo, email address and, if you added them, your phone number and FaceTime address;
  • everything you share in the app (moments, moods, letters once they open, plans, game answers once revealed, reactions and so on);
  • when you're online or last opened the app;
  • your approximate city and distance, only if location sharing is on;
  • your Gentle Days status or calendar, only if you turn sharing on.

Some things are deliberately hidden until the right moment, like a sealed letter before its opening time or a daily answer before both of you have replied.

Your partner can also save things you share (for example by taking screenshots, or saving a photo to their phone). We can't control copies made outside SYNC.

If you disconnect

  • Either of you can end the connection from Settings. When that happens, you're both unpaired and your shared space is archived: it's frozen and neither of you can see it.
  • While it's archived, each of you can download the things you created.
  • We keep the archive for 3 months, remind you about two weeks before it ends, and then delete it permanently.
  • Either of you can delete the shared space straight away from Settings, without waiting.
  • If the same two of you reconnect within those 3 months, we'll ask you both whether to bring your memories back or start fresh. They only come back if both of you agree.

6. Safety checks of photos

SYNC has a zero-tolerance rule on nudity and sexual or adult images, even between partners (see our Terms). To enforce it, every photo is checked automatically before your partner can see it.

  • Who does the checking: we do, on our own servers. Two free, open-source image-recognition models (Marqo's NSFW image classifier and NudeNet) run inside our server functions at Supabase, our hosting provider. No outside moderation company receives your photos, and the models don't learn from them.
  • How it works: a new photo first goes to a locked holding area that nobody can open, not even you or your partner. The check looks at it in memory. If it's fine, it moves into your shared space and your partner can see it. If it isn't, it's not shared. If the check can't run, nothing is shared and you can try again.
  • What counts: a photo that both models agree clearly shows nudity or sexual content is removed and counts as a warning. A photo where only one model is concerned is simply not shared, with no warning. Swimwear and underwear are allowed. Voice notes are only checked to make sure they really are audio. Videos can't be uploaded for now.
  • Warnings and bans: warnings don't expire. On a third warning your account is paused automatically, and a person on our team then reviews it and either confirms a ban or restores your account. A ban is never decided by a machine alone. You can ask for a review of any warning in the app or through support, give your point of view and contest the decision.
  • What we keep: for every photo that isn't shared we keep a minimal record: a digital fingerprint (hash) of the file, the models' scores and labels, the model version, the time, your account, your couple and which part of the app it was for. We don't keep the image, with two exceptions: (1) a photo that counts as a warning is kept in a locked store for 30 days, so that a person can look at it and handle any appeal, and is then deleted; (2) possible child sexual abuse material (below). For photos that are shared, we keep a short record that the check passed (fingerprint and time) until you delete your account.
  • Recognising banned people: so that warnings and bans can't be reset by making a new account, we keep scrambled versions (a keyed hash that can't be turned back into the original) of the email address, the Apple or Google sign-in ID and the app's install ID of anyone with a warning, pause or ban. A new account that matches is given the same status, and a person reviews it.
  • Child sexual abuse material: if an image may involve a child, we don't delete it. We preserve it and the related account information securely for at least one year, a person reviews it, and child sexual abuse material is reported to the authorities as the law requires: the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and the relevant Indian authorities. Such accounts are banned immediately. We record every time anyone on our team looks at preserved material.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete Account. Or follow the steps on our account deletion page. When you do:

  • everything you created (photos, videos, voice clips, notes, moods, answers, your profile and so on) is permanently deleted straight away. It isn't archived;
  • you're disconnected from your partner. Your partner's own contributions go into the archive described in section 5 and follow the 3-month rule;
  • a deleted account can't be restored. If you come back later, you'll start a new account;
  • copies in our backups disappear within 7 days;
  • we keep minimal safety records: warnings, pauses and bans with their scrambled identifiers (so they can't be reset by signing up again), records under an open appeal, and material we must preserve and report.

10. How we protect it

  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest by our hosting provider.
  • Database rules ("row-level security") make sure each account can only read its own data and its current partner's. Photos and files sit in private storage and are shown through short-lived links.
  • Sign-in is passwordless (Apple, Google or a one-time email code), so there are no passwords to steal. Your session is stored in your phone's secure keychain.
  • We email you when your account signs in on a new device or connects with someone.
  • Our backups are encrypted.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we'll notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, download a copy (portability), restrict or object to some processing, and withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect what we did before). Many of these you can do yourself in the app: edit your profile, turn off location sharing or Gentle Days sharing, change notification settings, disconnect, or delete your account. For anything else, email syncapp.noreply@gmail.com. We'll reply within one month (or sooner where the law requires), and may need to confirm it's really you first. You won't be treated differently for using your rights.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. For example: your local EU supervisory authority, the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), or the Data Protection Board of India. We'd appreciate the chance to help first.
